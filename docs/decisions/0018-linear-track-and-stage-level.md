# 0018 — Linear track and stage level I of X

Date: 2026-11-05  
Source: SuperGrok chat 05 / Page topic 4 (merge both)

## Decision
Keep the current linear tiles and UX quality bar. On English linear roads, add:
1. A continuous dark rail behind the stage's tiles (track feel without game chrome).
2. A "level I of X" chip per stage.

## Interim level rule
Until roads declare real per-stage levels in content, X is 3 and I is derived from tile completion in that stage: 1 below two-thirds done, 2 from two-thirds, 3 when the stage is complete. This is progress toward the stage goal, not points, streaks, or badges.

## Out of scope
Math 5-unit series UI (#248) stays on its own branch for Eliot's open decision. World-map rings for non-linear roads are unchanged.
